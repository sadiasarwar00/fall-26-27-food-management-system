How it works

Three simple steps.

  1. 1. Sign up

    Open Sign up, choose Customer, Waiter or Cashier and fill in your details.

  2. 2. Log in

    Use the login page for your type of user. Admin accounts are made by the owner.

  3. 3. Use Khabar Ghor

    Order and track food, serve tables or collect bills, depending on your account.
